# Changelog

All notable changes to **XpandedPortal** will be documented in this file.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- **Gear Energy Exemption**: Equipped weapons, armor, and tools no longer penalize portal teleport energy.
- **Multiplayer Group Conduit**: Traveling through an active portal opens a 30-second discount window for following party members with glowing rune visual cues.
- **Categorized Weight Accounting**: Granular configuration toggles for food, consumable, and ammunition portal energy costs.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- **Targeted Destination UI**: Interactive dropdown dial-a-destination portal selection menu (inspired by XPortal).
- **Portal Energy & Recharge System**: Configurable portal energy consumption scaling with carry weight and distance, with automatic equipment weight exemption.
- **Multiplayer Group Conduit**: Traveling through an active portal opens a 30-second discount window for following party members with glowing rune visuals.
- **Travel Restrictions & Safety**: Configurable item transport tiers, weight thresholds, transition speeds, and optional Teleportation Sickness debuff.
