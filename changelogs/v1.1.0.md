## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- **Gear Energy Exemption**: Equipped weapons, armor, and tools no longer penalize portal teleport energy.
- **Multiplayer Group Conduit**: Traveling through an active portal opens a 30-second discount window for following party members with glowing rune visual cues.
- **Categorized Weight Accounting**: Granular configuration toggles for food, consumable, and ammunition portal energy costs.
